# Lead generation by Tradeping

Lead generation for the local trades. Tradeping finds B2B leads from fresh job posts: roofing leads, HVAC leads, real estate leads and mortgage leads at companies hiring now, each with the decision maker, a verified email and a drafted first message for cold email outreach from your own mailbox.

## Connect with AI assistants

Our [MCP server](https://github.com/gettradeping/mcp-server) connects Tradeping to compatible AI clients, with browser sign-in and explicit permission.

get_account, list_leads and get_lead read your plan and feed. find_leads runs today's research within your plan's allowance (10 free verified leads to try it, then monthly on Starter and Pro). update_lead and rewrite_lead edit drafts; send_lead sends the next step from your own mailbox within your daily cap. list_suppressions and add_suppression manage your do-not-contact list.
